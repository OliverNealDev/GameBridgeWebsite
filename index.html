Events

Two things we run. Both are free.

Game Bridge

Our networking night at Teesside University Students’ Union. It’s relaxed, there’s no agenda, and it’s open to anyone working in games. Sponsors cover free drinks and a free return coach from Newcastle.

Where
Teesside University Students’ Union, Southfield Road, Middlesbrough TS1 3BZ
When
6pm till midnight, with a short welcome and talks at 8pm
Coach
Leaves Bewick Street, by Newcastle station, late afternoon and heads back at 11:30pm
Cost
Free. Register in advance to get drinks tokens and a coach seat
Who
Games industry, related industries, and graduate and final-year games students. 18+

GaMaYo Coach

GaMaYo (Game Makers Yorkshire) is a free gathering of more than 300 game makers from across the North, held twice a year in Leeds or Wakefield. We put on a free return coach so North East developers can get there and back.

From
Bewick Street, Newcastle, then Bridge Street West by Middlesbrough station
To
GaMaYo, and home again at the end of the night
Who
GaMaYo members with a ticket for the event itself
Cost
Free. Seats are limited, so book ahead

Booked a seat and can’t make it? Please cancel on Eventbrite so someone else can go.
