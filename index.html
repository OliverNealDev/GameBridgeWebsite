Game Bridge

Our networking night at Teesside University Students’ Union. It’s relaxed, there’s no agenda, and it’s open to anyone working in games. Sponsors cover free drinks and a free return coach from Newcastle.

Where
Terrace Bar, Teesside University Students’ Union, Southfield Road, Middlesbrough
When
6pm till midnight, with a short talk from hosts and sponsors at 8pm
Coach
Usually leaves Bewick Street, Newcastle, at 5pm and heads back at 11:30pm
Cost
Free. Register in advance to get the drinks and a coach seat
Who
Games industry, related industries and final-year games students. 18+

GaMaYo Coach

GaMaYo (Game Makers Yorkshire) is a free gathering of 300-odd game makers from across the North, held twice a year in Leeds or Wakefield. We put on a free return coach so North East developers can get there and back.

From
Newcastle and Middlesbrough
To
GaMaYo, and home again at the end of the night
Cost
Free. Seats are limited, so book ahead
